Day 1 JUNE 3 Rome, Italy
SUNNY MOOD
Trevi coin toss
first real carbonara
lost near the Pantheon
Landed at nine, napped for exactly zero minutes, and was immediately, gloriously lost. Rome does not care about your jetlag — it just hands you a gelato and points you toward the nearest fountain. Threw a coin in the Trevi (rules say I'm coming back, so, noted), ate carbonara that ruined every carbonara before it, and somehow ended up serenaded by a street violinist outside the Pantheon. Feet hurt. Heart very full.
I ate: cacio e pepe, three scoops of gelato
I saw: the Colosseum lit up gold at sunset
Favorite moment: that first bite of pizza al taglio, eaten standing up on a curb

Day 2 JUNE 4 Florence, Italy
SUNNY MOOD
Ponte Vecchio at golden hour
gelato, again
Trenitalia
Roma → Firenze
kept the ticket stub
Two hours on the train and suddenly everything is Renaissance. Spent the whole afternoon at the Uffizi getting genuinely overwhelmed — there is only so much beauty a person can process before lunch. Crossed the Ponte Vecchio three separate times because the light kept changing and I couldn't stop taking the same photo. Bought leather I absolutely do not need. No regrets.
I ate: bistecca alla fiorentina, way too much of it
I saw: Botticelli's Birth of Venus, in actual real life
Favorite moment: watching the sunset paint the Arno orange from the bridge

Day 3 JUNE 5 Venice, Italy
CLOUDY MOOD
canal-side, no destination
vaporetto views
There are no cars here and it changes everything about how a place sounds. Got beautifully, hopelessly lost in the back streets for two hours — every wrong turn led to a smaller, prettier canal. Rode the vaporetto up the Grand Canal like it was a bus, because it is one. Ended the day hopping between bacari for cicchetti and one very small glass of wine after another. Venice does not do anything quietly, except itself.
I ate: baccalà mantecato on crostini, more than I'll admit
I met: a gondolier who gave terrible, wonderful directions
Favorite moment: the quiet canal at dusk with not another soul around

Day 4 JUNE 6 Amalfi Coast, Italy
SUNNY MOOD
lemon groves forever
cliffside, no words
sunset from the boat
Last full day and the coastline is showing off. Took a tiny wooden boat out from the harbor and just floated for an hour, staring at cliffs that shouldn't be able to hold houses but somehow do. Bought a bag of lemons the size of my head from a roadside stand, ate lunch with my feet basically in the water, and watched the whole sky turn the exact color of the spritz in my hand. Already planning how to come back.
I ate: a lemon granita that reset my entire nervous system
I saw: Positano from the water, impossibly stacked on the cliff
Favorite moment: the boat engine cutting off and just… silence, and sea
